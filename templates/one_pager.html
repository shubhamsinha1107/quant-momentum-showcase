The idea

{{ sleeves|length }} rule-based strategies each hold at most one NSE index or metal ETF at a time, picked by recent risk-adjusted momentum on 30- or 60-minute bars and exited when that edge fades. A market-wide risk-off filter keeps them in cash in stress. Capital is split across the strategies and rebalanced monthly. No leverage, no shorting, no single stocks.

Method and limits

  • Same strategy code as the live paper-trading bot, run on index prices (30-min bars).
  • Positions marked at each day's close, open trades included; Sharpe on daily returns.
  • Rules built on {{ meta.start[:4] }}–{{ meta.rules_fixed_through[:4] }}: those years are in sample.
  • Indices aren't directly tradable; an ETF or futures would add tracking error.
  • Strategies can hold the same asset together, so concentration can be high.
  • Past model results don't predict future returns. Not investment advice.
