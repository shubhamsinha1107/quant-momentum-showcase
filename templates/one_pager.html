The Strategy in 60 Seconds
This strategy is an institutional-grade, multi-asset quantitative framework spanning Indian equities, sectoral indices, and precious metals. Rather than chasing single stocks or relying on passive buy-and-hold, it identifies institutional sector rotations, selects the strongest constituent stocks near 52-week highs, and automatically shifts into Gold/Silver ETFs and cash during market stress. Over a 6.8-year historical backtest (2020–2026), the simulated model delivered a 47.2% (Direct Index Baseline) to 73.0% (Multi-Asset Basket) CAGR while holding maximum drawdown to just -9.2% to -10.2% (2.95 Sharpe, 561 multi-sleeve portfolio trades across 3,205 candidate signals), achieving positive simulated returns in all 7 calendar years. Validated across an out-of-sample backtest (Jan 2023 – Jul 2025) and paper-traded forward since Aug 2025 (~14 months forward track record) with pilot allocations on personal capital via Zerodha & Fyers APIs to validate real-world execution.

1. The Problem with Retail Momentum & How We Solve It Strategy Thesis

Standard equity momentum strategies in emerging markets suffer from severe drawdown spikes (often -30% to -45%) during sudden market rotations, interest rate shifts, and choppy consolidation regimes. This framework resolves these vulnerabilities through three structural design principles:

Lead-Lag Sector Discovery (Top-Down Alpha)
Institutional capital in India enters broad industry sectors (Autos, Realty, PSU Banks, Metals, Energy) days before individual retail stocks react. By tracking trend inflection points on 30m and 60m intraday bars, the system enters emerging trends early with high statistical probability.
Multi-Asset Shock Absorbers (Equities + Physical Gold & Silver ETFs)
Equities do not operate in isolation. When market-wide equity momentum breaks down, capital automatically routes to Physical Gold (GOLDBEES) and Silver (SILVERBEES) ETFs and liquid cash, turning risk-off equity selloffs into uncorrelated crisis alpha.
The Multi-Sleeve Diversification Dividend
Single-horizon strategies suffer deep peak-to-trough drawdowns (-13.6% to -23.3%). Mathematically combining complementary sleeves—fast tactical momentum (30m) with core trend (60m) and defensive hedging—compresses portfolio drawdown to just -7.1% to -10.2%.

2. Three Operating Sleeves: How Capital is Allocated Architecture

The master portfolio is split into three disciplined, rules-driven capital sleeves:

Sleeve 1: Rapid Sector Momentum (50% AUM | 30-min Horizon)
Captures fast-moving rotational swings across cyclical sectors and precious metals. Uses an adaptive volatility trail to enter confirmed breakouts, filtered by a 40-day trend efficiency gate (Kaufman Efficiency Ratio ≥ 0.20) that deliberately prevents trading in noisy, sideways markets.
Sleeve 2: Enhanced Core Trend (25% AUM | 60-min Horizon)
Captures sustained multi-week institutional trends. Rather than equal weighting, constituent stocks are sized using inverse-volatility risk parity (allocating more capital to lower-volatility leaders), ensuring volatile smallcaps do not disproportionately drive portfolio risk.
Sleeve 3: Multi-Asset Crash Hedge (25% AUM | 60-min Horizon)
Monitors mid/smallcap quality champions alongside Gold and Silver ETFs. When equity breadth breaks down, defensive assets activate automatically, dampening overall portfolio volatility.

4. Why the Backtest is Real Anti-Overfitting

Point-In-Time (PIT) Index Mapping
Constituent universes are synced to historical semi-annual NSE index changes (Mar/Sep), completely eliminating survivorship and look-ahead bias.
Parameter Stability (No Knife-Edge Tuning)
Testing constituent basket sizes from 1 to 10 stocks demonstrates a broad, stable Sharpe plateau (2.45 to 2.63) rather than relying on a fragile, overfitted single parameter.
Realistic Transaction Drag Modeled
Tested under 15–20 bps round-trip friction (STT, exchange turnover, stamp duty, slippage). Frictional drag consumes only ~10%–12% of gross trade expectancy.
