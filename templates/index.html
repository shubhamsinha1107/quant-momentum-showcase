2. Execution & Friction Model

Signal Timing: Signals evaluated on 30m/60m bar close; orders filled at next bar open tick (1-bar execution delay).

15–20 bps Round-Trip Friction: 0.10% STT (sell), 0.00345% NSE exchange fee, 0.015% stamp duty, and 0.05% execution slippage. 100% Cash equity (no margin/leverage).

3. Tradability & Implementation

Vehicles: Sectoral implementation via liquid NSE ETFs (NIFTYBEES, BANKBEES, ITBEES, GOLDBEES, SILVERBEES) or liquid index futures.

Proxy Synthesis: For indices lacking dedicated high-volume ETFs, top-3 heavyweights are held as tracking proxies with minimal tracking error.

4. Limitations & Capacity

Capacity: Estimated at ₹25–50 Cr ($3M–$6M) before market impact on smaller mid/smallcap constituent liquidity manifests.

Regime Risk: Prone to whipsaw in low-volatility sideways ranges. Tax treated under Section 43(5) of the Indian Income Tax Act.
